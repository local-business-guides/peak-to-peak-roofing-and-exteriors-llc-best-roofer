What the job can include

A roofing project may cover a limited repair, a replacement, or another agreed scope. The actual work and duration depend on the roof’s condition, the size of the area, material selection, access, and scheduling. Cost is also shaped by those project details, so a general description cannot establish what your particular home will require.

Use this key-facts list to frame the discussion:
- Work area: Identify the roof section or issue being addressed.
- Included tasks: Ask what labor and materials the scope accounts for.
- Schedule: Find out the expected order of work and what could affect timing.
- Site handling: Discuss access and cleanup arrangements.
- Coordination: Ask if gutters, siding, or solar work could affect the roofing plan.

To compare providers, review how completely each one defines the work, names the proposed materials, and answers questions about timing and cleanup. Also check that you are comparing similar scopes. A *roof deck* is the base surface supporting the roof covering. *Flashing* helps protect joints and transitions from water entry. These terms may come up when a contractor describes roof details.
