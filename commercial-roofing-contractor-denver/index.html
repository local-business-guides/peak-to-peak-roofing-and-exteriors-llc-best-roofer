Scope, schedule, cost factors, and roofing terms

Commercial roof projects vary by building, existing materials, and requested work. A typical process involves discussing the concern, assessing the roof, defining the proposed scope, and planning the work. Timing isn’t fixed: project size, access, material availability, weather, and coordination needs can affect the schedule.

Key facts to review:

  • What it covers: The agreed roof work. Ask whether related gutters, siding, or solar equipment are part of the discussion or outside the scope.
  • What affects cost: Roof area and layout, materials, repair versus replacement, access, and related work can all matter. Request property-specific written details rather than relying on a broad estimate.
  • What to clarify: Proposed materials, work areas, exclusions, site access, cleanup, and how schedule updates will be shared.

A roof deck is the structural surface beneath the roofing materials. A membrane is a sheet-like roofing layer used on some roof systems. Flashing helps direct water around joints, edges, and openings. Ask providers to explain any system-specific terms in their proposal.
